## 📖 Project Overview

AtliQ Hotels is a hotel chain operating **25 hotels** across major cities in India. Despite being a well-established brand, the company has experienced a decline in its market share. This project analyzes booking and revenue data from **April to July 2022** to identify the key factors affecting business performance and provide actionable insights.

The dashboard focuses on booking trends, revenue generation, cancellation patterns, city-wise performance, and hotel-level analysis to support strategic business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this analysis is to understand why AtliQ Hotels is experiencing a decline in revenue and bookings by:

- Identifying booking and revenue trends
- Analyzing cancellation behavior
- Comparing city-wise performance
- Detecting hotels with unusually high cancellation rates
- Providing recommendations for improving business performance

---

# 📌 Major Findings

- Revenue is strongly correlated with booking volume.
- Overall booking volume declined throughout the analysis period.
- Overall cancellation percentage improved.
- Certain hotels showed unusually high cancellation rates in later weeks.
- Additional customer feedback data is required to identify the root cause behind declining bookings.

---

# 💡 Recommendations

- Investigate hotels with high cancellation rates.
- Monitor weekly cancellation trends.
- Improve food, hospitality, and hotel management where necessary.
- Strengthen marketing campaigns to increase bookings.
- Perform sentiment analysis on customer reviews to uncover service issues.

# 📈 Conclusion

The analysis shows that the decline in revenue is primarily driven by a reduction in bookings. While the overall cancellation rate has improved over time, a few specific hotels continue to experience unusually high cancellation percentages and require further investigation.

To better understand the underlying causes of declining bookings, additional data such as customer reviews, service quality metrics, and guest feedback would be valuable. These insights can help AtliQ Hotels identify operational issues, improve customer satisfaction, reduce cancellations, and ultimately regain market share.
